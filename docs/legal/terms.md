# Podcast Studio – Terms of Service

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Podcast Studio is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Hanh Le. It publishes short vertical
video clips of the operator's own podcast to the operator's own TikTok account.

1. **Who can use it.** Podcast Studio is used only by its operator. It is not offered to the public
   and does not accept sign-ups.
2. **What it does.** After the operator approves a new podcast episode, Podcast Studio uploads that
   episode's short clips to the operator's TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.
   It does not read, collect or publish content from any other TikTok account.
3. **Content.** All published content is created and owned by the operator, who is responsible
   for making sure it follows TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
4. **Access.** The operator can disconnect Podcast Studio at any time from TikTok
   (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
